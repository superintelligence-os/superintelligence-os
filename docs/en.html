Android with the AI you choose as the system.

SIOS is an operating system for Android phones where an AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Kimi, Mistral, Grok, any API or a model running on the phone itself) does things for you: in your apps, in your notifications and with your stuff. Open source and truly private.

See the codePrivacy policy
Your AI, your choiceSwitch whenever you like. With no AI chosen, SIOS stays neutral.
Your stuff stays on the phoneVault, memory, dictation, translation and photo text never leave it.
No trackersPer-app firewall and ad blocking built in.
Find your phoneFrom your computer, end-to-end encrypted, no accounts.

Status

Version 0.1, in development. Tested on the Android emulator; not yet tested on real phones. Want to help? See how to contribute.
